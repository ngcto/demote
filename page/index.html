Demote

Move credentials that fail with selected HTTP codes to the back of their provider’s saved order.

Setup: enable authenticated Browser access in Settings → Network and sharing. Demote only connects over loopback. It never enables Browser access for you.

Demotions persist. No accounts are deleted or disabled. Reordering subscription accounts can switch the agent’s primary sign-in; Magpie’s Keep login setting prevents that. Turning Demote off stops new changes but does not undo saved orders or routing.
